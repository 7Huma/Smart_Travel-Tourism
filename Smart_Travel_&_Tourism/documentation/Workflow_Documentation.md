# Workflow Documentation

## Workflow 1 – Customer Registration & Preference Collection

Collects traveler information including name, email, destination preferences, budget, travel dates, and interests. The workflow validates user inputs and stores the collected information for further processing.

---

## Workflow 2 – Destination Recommendation & Budget Planning

Uses OpenAI to recommend suitable travel destinations based on customer interests, travel budget, preferred activities, and trip duration.

---

## Workflow 3 – Booking Management & Itinerary Generation

Creates a personalized travel itinerary by organizing transportation, hotel bookings, sightseeing plans, and daily schedules.

---

## Workflow 4 – Travel Alerts & Notifications

Automatically sends travel reminders, booking confirmations, weather updates, and important travel notifications through Gmail.

---

## Workflow 5 – Feedback Collection & Travel Analytics

Collects customer feedback after the trip and stores responses for future analysis and service improvement.

---

## Workflow 6 – Travel Feedback Analytics

Analyzes customer feedback to identify travel trends, customer satisfaction levels, and improvement opportunities for travel agencies.

---
