# Problem Analysis

## Project Title
Smart Tourism & Travel Planning Platform

## Business Context

Travel agencies often spend significant time understanding customer preferences, comparing destinations, coordinating bookings, preparing itineraries, and communicating travel updates manually. These repetitive tasks reduce operational efficiency and make it difficult to provide personalized travel experiences to multiple customers simultaneously.

This project automates the complete travel planning process using n8n workflows and AI-powered recommendations, improving efficiency and customer satisfaction.

---

## Stakeholders

- Travel Agencies
- Travel Consultants
- Customers / Travelers
- Hotel & Transportation Providers

---

## Pain Points

- Manual customer registration
- Time-consuming itinerary planning
- Difficulty in comparing travel destinations
- Repetitive email communication
- Lack of personalized travel recommendations
- Manual follow-up for customer feedback

---

## Objectives

- Automate customer registration
- Recommend destinations using AI
- Generate personalized itineraries
- Manage bookings efficiently
- Send automated travel reminders
- Collect customer feedback
- Generate travel analytics reports
